### 👋 About Me

I'm a **PhD student** at the [Institute of Cancer Research](https://www.icr.ac.uk/), supervised by [Professor Chris Bakal](https://www.icr.ac.uk/our-research/researchers-and-teams/professor-chris-bakal), and a **Research Scientist** at [Sentinal4D](https://www.sentinal4d.com) — a London-based AI startup building next-generation 3D cell modelling technology.

My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning**, **computational biology**, and **biophysics** — using generative AI and computer vision to decode how cancer cells look, move, and respond to drugs in 3D.

My PhD is funded by the [NIHR BRC Studentship](https://www.maudsleybrc.nihr.ac.uk/academic-career-development/current-opportunities/phd-studentships-2023/) award.
